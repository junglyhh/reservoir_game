A MOMENT TO FIND YOUR BALANCE

Small moves.
Beautiful possibilities.

One source of light. A connected garden. Every drop matters.

01

Tap anywhere along a connection

The two vessels share their light equally. The source gives up light too. A connection that is already balanced uses no move.

02

Meet every target

Bring all marked vessels to their goal at the same time, within the move limit. A lit flower can dim if you share its light again.

03

Explore freely

Undo restores your light and your move. Restart whenever you like. Aim for the elegant solution to earn a golden bloom.

Keyboard: Tab to a connection, Enter to share · Z to undo · R to restart

THE IDEA AT THE ROOT

An elegant limit.

Start with one unit of light at a source. Choose any connection and replace the amounts at its two ends with their average.

Maximum possible limit = 1 / (distance + 1)

A vessel two connections from the source can approach one third of the total light. Repeated averaging may approach this limit without ever reaching it exactly.

Our levels use reachable goals and finite move limits. Several goals must be met together, so their individual ceilings do not guarantee a shared solution.
