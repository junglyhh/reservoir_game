A MOMENT TO FIND YOUR BALANCE

Small moves.
Beautiful possibilities.

One source of light. A connected garden. Every drop matters.

01

Tap anywhere along a connection

The amounts of light at the two ends are averaged. The source follows the same rule. If both ends already have equal amounts of light, tapping the connection does not use a move.

02

Meet or exceed every target

Every target percentage shown in the game is a required minimum. A vessel meets its target when its current percentage of light is greater than or equal to the target. Exact equality is not required. All targets must be met at the same time within the move limit. A lit bloom can go out if you share its light again. A check mark appears next to a level once you clear it. Clear it in the fewest possible moves to earn a golden bloom.

03

Clear a level to unlock the next

Clear each level within its move limit to unlock the next one; you do not need a golden bloom first. You can also take on the ultimate challenge: earn a golden bloom on every level. The final four Master levels have move limits equal to their minimums, so every successful clear earns a golden bloom.

Keyboard: Tab to a connection, Enter to share · Z to undo · R to restart

THE IDEA AT THE ROOT

An elegant limit.

Start with one unit of light at a source. Choose any connection and replace the amounts at its two ends with their average.

Maximum possible limit = 1 / (distance + 1)

A vessel two connections from the source can approach one third of the total light. Repeated averaging may approach this limit without ever reaching it exactly.

Our levels use reachable minimum targets and finite move limits. Meeting or exceeding a target counts; exact equality is not needed. All targets must be met together, so their individual ceilings do not guarantee a shared solution.

The ultimate challenge: sharing tea

Imagine a cup at every vertex of a connected graph. Put one unit of tea at the root r and leave every other cup empty. Each move chooses an edge and shares its two cups equally.

Tea at vertex x ≤ 1 / (d(r, x) + 1)

Here d(r, x) is the shortest-path distance from the root to x. Can you prove this bound for every connected graph? Can you explain when it can be reached exactly and when it can only be approached?
